**Subject:** LRS3 access and usage permissions for Project Echo

Dear LRS3 maintainers,

I am preparing Project Echo, a communication assistant for people with speech impairments, and am reviewing the official access and usage terms for LRS3 before deciding whether the dataset is suitable. The KAIST page describes LRS3 as available for research under CC BY 4.0 and notes that copyright remains with the original video owners. Could you please clarify the following?

1. Is registration, an application, or explicit approval required to access the LRS3 videos and transcripts? If so, what is the official procedure and current download location?
2. May LRS3 be used in a time-limited hackathon prototype, including a prototype demonstrated to judges or other attendees?
3. May LRS3 be used to develop or evaluate a model that could later be included in a commercial product, even if dataset access and model development initially occur for research or prototyping?
4. May derived MediaPipe facial/lip landmarks, annotations, and model weights trained using LRS3 be retained and used commercially? Are there separate terms for these derived artifacts?
5. What restrictions apply to redistribution of the original videos, transcripts, clips, or other dataset files? Are there limits on sharing derived data or model outputs that could reproduce, identify, or expose source content?
6. Does the CC BY 4.0 statement apply to all dataset components, or are the TED/TEDx video and transcript materials subject to additional permissions or terms from their original owners?

We will not download or use LRS3 until access requirements and applicable permissions are clear. Thank you for your guidance.

Best regards,

Project Echo team

## Verification context

- Official KAIST dataset page: <https://mm.kaist.ac.kr/datasets/lip_reading/>
- Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 deed: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>
- Oxford VGG publication listing the LRS3 authors: <https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/publications/2018/Afouras18b/afouras18b.pdf>

The KAIST page's indexed content states research download availability, CC BY 4.0, and that video copyright remains with original owners. Direct retrieval of the KAIST and Oxford LRS3 dataset pages timed out during this verification, so live links, access/approval workflow, and any component-specific terms remain unverified. CC BY 4.0 generally permits commercial reuse of material to which it applies, subject to attribution and other terms; that does not resolve whether all LRS3 video/transcript components are covered or whether third-party rights apply.
